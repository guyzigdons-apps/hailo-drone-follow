<objective>
Create three rover-adapted actor worlds under `sim/rover/worlds/` — `walk_across_then_approach.sdf`, `random_walk.sdf`, and `circle_around.sdf`. Each world spawns the rover (via `<include><uri>model://rover</uri></include>`, consuming Plan 05-01's output) and an animated `<actor>` walking through a trajectory adapted from the corresponding drone-sim world in `sim/worlds/`.

Address RSIM-03 (three rover-adapted actor worlds) and Phase 5 Success Criterion 4 ("at least one rover actor world — `walk_across_then_approach` — renders a walking person; rover model appears on a ground plane").

**Adaptation rules (from RESEARCH.md § "Adapting drone worlds for rover"):**
- Drone worlds spawn the drone via PX4's `PX4_GZ_WORLD` mechanism (no `<include>` of the drone in the SDF). Rover worlds DO include the rover model directly: `<include><uri>model://rover</uri><pose>0 0 0.15 0 0 0</pose></include>`.
- Drop drone-specific world plugins (`gz::sim::systems::Imu`, `gz::sim::systems::AirPressure`) — rover has no IMU / barometer needs. Keep `Physics`, `UserCommands`, `SceneBroadcaster`, `Sensors` (`Sensors` is required for the rover's onboard camera). `Contact` is optional; keep for now to allow Phase 6 wheel-contact debugging.
- Compress actor trajectory X-coordinates by ~0.4× (drone sees from 3 m altitude with wide FOV; rover sees from 0.20 m altitude with same FOV → actor must enter the camera frame at a shorter forward distance). Actor Y-range can stay the same (rover yaws to track lateral motion).
- Keep actor `<pose>` Z = 1.0 m (eye-level mesh anchor). Tuning is deferred to Phase 6 RINT-04 if the actor renders below frame.
- Copy `<physics>`, `<scene>`, `<light name="sunUTC">`, `<ground_plane>`, `<spherical_coordinates>` verbatim from the source drone world — they are world-rendering boilerplate.

This plan is autonomous, runs in Wave 1, and can run **in parallel** with Plan 05-01 (rover.sdf) and Plan 05-03 (README). All three touch disjoint files. The rover model itself does not need to exist yet at the time these worlds are committed — Gazebo resolves `model://rover` at sim launch via `GZ_SIM_RESOURCE_PATH`, not at SDF parse time.

Output: Three SDF files under `sim/rover/worlds/`. Verification is structural (grep) + optional `gz sdf -k` skip-on-no-gz lint. The "actor actually walks and rover renders on ground plane" verification is Phase 6 RINT-04 under operator gate.
